Niagara Succession Signal

DataraFlow Founder Challenge 2026

Mapping small-business succession risk in Niagara Region, Ontario

---

Project Overview

The Niagara Succession Signal is a data-driven research project investigating the risk of small businesses closing because owners approach retirement without a successor or buyer.

The project focuses on understanding:

- Which types of small businesses face succession risk
- How risk varies across sectors and municipalities
- How many jobs could be exposed
- Where succession risk is concentrated
- What actions could help businesses prepare for succession, acquisition, or transition

The project combines public business, demographic, employment, and business-dynamics data with research and forecasting methods to produce practical evidence for Niagara stakeholders.

---

Core Research Question

«Which small businesses in Niagara Region are most exposed to succession-related closure risk, how many jobs could be affected, where is the risk concentrated, and what actions could reduce the potential impact?»

---

Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Build an aggregated data layer for analyzing Niagara's small-business landscape.
2. Investigate business succession and closure risk using available evidence.
3. Identify sectoral and geographic patterns in potential succession risk.
4. Estimate employment exposure associated with businesses at risk.
5. Develop a forecasting model for business closures or at-risk business counts.
6. Develop a generic business acquisition and succession playbook.
7. Analyze potential local-economic impacts.
8. Develop practical remediation recommendations for government and private-sector stakeholders.

Reproducibility

Reproducibility is a core principle of this project.

The project will aim to:

- Document data sources and versions
- Record important data transformations
- Use fixed random seeds where appropriate
- Avoid undocumented manual processing
- Document assumptions and limitations
- Maintain version-controlled code
- Provide sufficient information for another researcher to understand and reproduce the analysis

---

Ethics & Data Protection

The project will follow the data and research requirements of the DataraFlow Founder Challenge.

The analysis will use aggregated or appropriately permitted data and will not identify, profile, or single out individual business owners or named businesses.

Confidential, restricted, or partner-only information will not be published or placed in the repository unless the applicable terms explicitly permit it.

The project will report uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations rather than presenting forecasts as certain outcomes.

The project does not provide specific transaction advice for individual businesses.
