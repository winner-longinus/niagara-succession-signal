Data

Purpose

This folder contains the datasets used in the Niagara Succession Signal project.

The data stored here will support the analysis of small-business succession risk, business dynamics, employment exposure, sector patterns, geographic concentration, and forecasting.

What Belongs Here

This folder may contain:

- Publicly available datasets
- Properly licensed project data
- Cleaned and processed datasets
- Data dictionaries and metadata
- Other approved datasets required for the project

Expected Data Sources

Potential sources include:

- Statistics Canada Business Counts
- Statistics Canada Business Dynamics
- Statistics Canada Census and demographic data
- Niagara regional datasets
- CFIB research data where permitted
- Other approved project datasets

Data Organization

Where necessary, datasets may be organized into:

- "raw/" — original datasets obtained from approved sources
- "processed/" — cleaned and transformed datasets
- "external/" — external datasets used in the analysis

Data Protection

Confidential, private, personally identifiable, restricted, or partner-only data must not be uploaded to this repository unless the applicable data-sharing terms explicitly permit it.

The project should use aggregated data where required by the challenge rules and must not identify or profile individual business owners or named businesses.

Reproducibility

Important datasets should have their source, access date, relevant variables, licensing or access conditions, and major transformations documented.

The goal is for another researcher to understand where the data came from and how it was prepared for analysis.

Relationship to the Project

The data in this folder provides the foundation for:

- Exploratory analysis
- Business succession-risk analysis
- Geographic and sector analysis
- Employment-impact analysis
- Forecasting
- Visualizations
- Policy and remediation recommendations
