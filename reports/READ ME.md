Reports

Purpose

This folder contains the formal written deliverables produced for the Niagara Succession Signal project.

Reports translate the project's data, analysis, models, findings, and evidence into structured documents that can be communicated to judges, stakeholders, policymakers, business-support organizations, and other intended audiences.

Expected Deliverables

Depending on the final project scope, this folder may contain:

- Research report
- Local-economy impact analysis
- Government and private-sector remediation report
- Business acquisition and succession playbook
- Policy brief
- Other approved written deliverables

Report Development

Reports should be developed from the project's documented analysis and supporting evidence.

Important findings should be traceable to:

- Relevant datasets
- Analytical methods
- Outputs
- Statistical or forecasting results
- Documented assumptions

Working Principles

Reports should clearly distinguish between:

- Observed data
- Analytical findings
- Forecasts and model estimates
- Assumptions
- Uncertainty
- Limitations
- Recommendations

Forecasts should not be presented as certain outcomes, and limitations should be communicated clearly.

Relationship to the Project

The "reports/" folder represents the project's formal communication layer.

The analytical work performed in the notebooks and source code produces evidence in "outputs/". That evidence is then interpreted and communicated through the formal reports stored here.

In simple terms:

Analysis produces evidence → Reports explain what the evidence means.
